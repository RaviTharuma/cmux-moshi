## What this project is not

- Not official cmux or Moshi support
- Not a place to report bugs in cmux, Moshi, tmux, or mosh themselves (file
  those upstream)
- Not a general terminal or SSH consulting channel

## Response expectations

This is a small maintained project. Issues and PRs are handled as maintainer
time allows. Clear reproduction steps and `cmux-moshi doctor` output speed
things up a lot.
