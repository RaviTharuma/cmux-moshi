## What this project is not

- Not official cmux or Moshi support
- Not a place to report bugs in cmux, Moshi, tmux, or mosh themselves (file
  those upstream)
- Not a general terminal or SSH consulting channel
- **Not a warranty, insurance policy, or indemnity** for supply-chain attacks,
  contributor malware, host damage, or any cloud/CI/AI **credit or spend**
  overages on your accounts

## Response expectations

This is a small maintained project. Issues and PRs are handled as maintainer
time allows. There is **no support SLA**. Clear reproduction steps and
`cmux-moshi doctor` output speed things up a lot.

Answering an issue or merging a PR does **not** create liability or a duty to
audit further changes. See [TERMS.md](TERMS.md).
